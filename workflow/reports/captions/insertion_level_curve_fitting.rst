Insertion-level fitted curves (sampled)
========================================

Sigmoid curve fits for a random sample of insertions. Each panel shows
observed LFC values (points) and the fitted sigmoid curve (line) for one
insertion, with R² and RMSE goodness-of-fit metrics. Panel titles display
insertion position (Chr:Coordinate Strand) and, when available, the gene
or genomic feature the insertion falls within. Curves are sampled randomly
(seed 42) from successful fits to give a representative view of model
quality across the dataset.
