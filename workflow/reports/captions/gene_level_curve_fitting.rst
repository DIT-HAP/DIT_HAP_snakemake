Gene-level fitted curves (sampled)
===================================

Sigmoid curve fits for a random sample of genes. Each panel shows
observed LFC values (points) and the fitted sigmoid curve (line) for one
gene, with R² and RMSE goodness-of-fit metrics. Panel titles display
the gene's systematic ID, common name, viability status, and deletion
library classification. Curves are sampled randomly (seed 42) from
successful fits to give a representative view of model quality across
the gene-level dataset.
